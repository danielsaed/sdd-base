# History — every closed spec

> **Answers:** what was done, in which area and in which PR, newest first. **Update
> when:** a spec closes or a docs-mode PR is opened: one line at the TOP, in the PR's
> last commit. Never edited afterwards. The spec itself: `areas/<area>/done/` (last 3
> per area) or main's history (`areas/README.md`, «History with balance»).
> Format: `YYYY-MM-DD · <area>/<slug> · what (one line) · PR #N` (docs mode: `docs/<topic>`).
